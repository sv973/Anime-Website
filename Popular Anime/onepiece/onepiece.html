📜 The Pirate's Tale

One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy, a fearless young pirate who dreams of becoming the Pirate King. With his crew of loyal adventurers, Luffy sails through dangerous waters, mysterious islands, powerful enemies, and legendary battles while searching for the treasure known as the One Piece.
